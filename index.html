Featured Worlds

Every structure begins with a story.

Our signature worlds are starting points, not catalog items. Each one is reimagined around your child, your room, and your home's architecture.

  • The Alchemist's Attic

    A peaked loft of copper, glass, and star charts, where shelves of curious bottles hold crayons instead of potions and every round window looks out on a new experiment.

  • The Mid-Century Meadow

    Clean, slanted lines and walnut warmth meet a wildflower palette, a playhouse that feels at home beside an Eames chair while still hiding a secret reading nook.

  • The Fox's Hollow

    A hand-carved tree-trunk den with an arched door and a lantern-lit burrow inside, the cozy hideaway where our namesake fox would keep its favorite fables.

  • The Cloud Atelier

    A blush-and-ivory bunk room for little artists, with stacked cloud berths, a rope-and-rail ladder, and a skylit studio corner for painting the day's daydreams.

The Story-First Process

From first sketch to first adventure.

  1. 01

    Design About 2 weeks

    We start with a consultation to learn your child's imagination and your home's style, then present a 3D rendering of the structure in your actual room.

  2. 02

    Workshop Build 4–6 weeks

    Your world is crafted locally in our Kaysville workshop on a modular frame, with joinery, finishes, and hand-painted details done by hand.

  3. 03

    Installation Typically 1–3 days

    Our carpenters assemble everything on-site, complete final safety checks, and deliver a curated Story Kit of books and toys matched to your theme.

Why Fox & Fable

Built like architecture. Loved like a storybook.

Our mission is to build worlds within worlds that foster creativity, independence, and aesthetic harmony in the modern home. A Kaysville, Utah company, we design and build every piece for families across Davis County and Northern Utah.

  • Artisan craftsmanship

    Solid hardwoods, real joinery, and hand-finished details made to be passed down.

  • Non-toxic sustainability

    Responsibly sourced wood and low-VOC, child-safe paints and finishes.

  • Child-centered safety

    Rounded edges, secure anchoring, and a final safety check on every install.

  • Architectural integrity

    Designed to your room's proportions so it reads as part of the house, not an add-on.

Marked by hand. Each piece will carry our brand, pressed into the wood with a custom iron, so the story of who made it stays with it for the next generation.
